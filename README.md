# Awn 🤝

> Give with Purpose. Follow the Impact.

## 📝 About The Project

Awn is a mobile platform that connects donors with verified individuals and families in need. Instead of making general donations, users can choose specific needs such as medical treatment, education expenses, or essential devices.

The platform provides transparency throughout the donation journey, allowing donors to track their contribution and see its impact until the specific need is fulfilled.
